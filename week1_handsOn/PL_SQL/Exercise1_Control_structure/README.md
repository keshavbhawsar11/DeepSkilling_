# Exercise 1 - Control Structures

## Objective

Implement PL/SQL control structures such as FOR LOOP and IF statements to perform banking operations on customer and loan data.

---

## Scenarios

### Scenario 1

Apply a 1% discount to the loan interest rate of customers whose age is above 60 years.

### Scenario 2

Promote customers with a balance greater than $10,000 to VIP status by updating the IsVIP flag.

### Scenario 3

Generate reminder messages for customers whose loans are due within the next 30 days.

---

## Concepts Used

- PL/SQL Block
- FOR LOOP
- IF Statement
- UPDATE Statement
- SELECT Statement
- DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE
- DATE Functions
- MONTHS_BETWEEN()
- COMMIT

---

## Files

- solution.sql

---

## Expected Output

### Scenario 1

Customers above 60 years receive a 1% reduction in their loan interest rate.

### Scenario 2

Customers with a balance greater than $10,000 are marked as VIP.

### Scenario 3

Reminder messages are displayed for customers whose loan due date falls within the next 30 days.

---

## Conclusion

This exercise demonstrates the use of PL/SQL control structures to automate banking operations using conditional statements, loops, and database updates.
